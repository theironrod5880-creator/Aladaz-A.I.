# The Patriarchal Mirrors

## The 75-Year Father-Son Handoff
· Terah dies at 205 years old (Genesis 11:32). At the time of Terah's death, Abraham is exactly 75 years old (Acts 7:4).
· Abraham dies at 175 years old (Genesis 25:7). Isaac was born when Abraham was 100. Therefore, at the exact moment Abraham dies, Isaac is exactly 75 years old.

## The Lifespan of Isaac (Half a Time)
· Isaac lived to be 180 years old (Genesis 35:28).
· 180 is exactly half of a prophetic year (360 ÷ 2 = 180).
· When Daniel 7:25 speaks of "a time, times, and half a time" (3.5 times): 3 times (3 × 360) = 1,080. + half a time (180) = 1,260.
· Isaac's life spans the exact numerical value of the great prophetic half-week.

## The 120-Year Handoff
· Isaac was 60 years old when Jacob was born (Genesis 25:26).
· Isaac lived to be 180 years old (Genesis 35:28).
· 180 - 60 = 120. Jacob was exactly 120 years old when Isaac died.
· 120 is the master number: 120 years before the Flood, 120 Jubilees (5,880 years), the 120-year final epoch (1914-2034).

## The 28-Year Marker
· 2080 years back from Isaac lands on 3998 B.C.E. — which is exactly 28 years after Adam was created (4026 B.C.E.).
· 1080 years back from Isaac lands on 2998 B.C.E. — which is exactly 28 years before Noah was born.
· The distance between those two points is exactly 1,000 years.
