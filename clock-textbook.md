## PREFACE: THE FOUR-TIER STANDARD
This book examines the intersection of scripture, mathematics, and astronomy. To maintain integrity, every claim is classified:
- Tier 1: Established textual or scientific fact.
- Tier 2: Verified mathematical calculation.
- Tier 3: Plausible interpretation (the author's reading).
- Tier 4: Unresolved hypothesis or open question.

## Chapter 1: The 5,880-Year Wheel
The timeline is a wheel, not a line.
- The Span: From the Fall of man (3967 B.C.E.) to the end of the Gentile Times (1914 C.E.) is exactly 5,880 years. (There is no year zero: 3967 + 1914 - 1 = 5,880). (Tier 2)
- The Center: 2,940 years from each end lands on 1,027 B.C.E. - the completion of Solomon's temple. (Tier 2)
- The Epochs: 5,880 = 7 × 840 (Seven epochs of 840 years). (Tier 2)
- The Cycles: 5,880 = 120 × 49 (120 Jubilees). (Tier 2)

## Chapter 2: The 60-Year Paradox (Two Adams)
There are two starting dates: 4026 B.C.E. and 3967 B.C.E. The resolution is the 60-year gap between Adam's creation and the Fall. (Tier 3)
- 4026 B.C.E.: Adam created (6th creative day).
- 3967 B.C.E.: 6th day ends (autumn). God blesses the 7th day. The clock starts here.
- 3966 B.C.E.: 1st full year of the fall completes.
- The 59 years from 4026 to 3967 is the time Adam spent in the 6th day. The 1 year from 3967 to 3966 completes the 60-year span. (Tier 2)
- The Prophetic Drama (Terah's Sons): Terah = Jehovah. Haran (firstborn) = Adam after the fall. He died in Ur (land of his origin), as Adam was expelled from Eden. Dates to the Fall (3966). (Tier 3)
- Abraham (youngest) = The line carrying Genesis 3:15 (the 2nd Adam). He entered Canaan (promised land), as Adam entered Eden. Dates to the beginning (4026). (Tier 3)
- The Shadow (1883 B.C.E.): Without Acts 7:4, the Genesis text yields 1883 B.C.E. as Abraham's entry. The 60-year gap (1943 to 1883) mirrors the Genesis gap (4026 to 3966). The offset between the frames is exactly 2,083 years. (Tier 2)

## Chapter 3: The 105 Hinge & The 450 Years
The number 105 is the foundational unit of the architecture. (Tier 2/3)
- Abraham: 75 at the crossing + 30 (430 years minus 400 years) = 105 at Isaac's weaning.
- The Solar Gap: 40 solar years (365.25) vs 40 prophetic years (360) = 210 days (2 × 105).
- The 400-Year Gap: 400 solar years vs 400 prophetic years = 2,100 days (20 × 105).
- The 450-Year Judges (Acts 13:20): If Paul's 450 years of Judges is taken literally, the Temple foundation moves from 1034 B.C.E. to 929 B.C.E. The difference is exactly 105 years. (Tier 2)

## Chapter 4: The 49,000-Year Week
(Recovered from the Watchtower's abandoned teaching).
- 6 creative days × 7,000 years = 42,000 years.
- 7th day (7,000 years) = 49,000 years.
- After the 1,000-year reign, Jesus hands the kingdom back to the Father - this pictures the Jubilee (the 50,000th year). (Tier 3)
