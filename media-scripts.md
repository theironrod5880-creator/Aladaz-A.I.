# The Ezekiel 1:16 Wheels Video

## Technical Script Overview
This is a Python script designed to generate a 45-second cinematic video. It uses the `moviepy` library to render frames, `matplotlib` to draw the visuals, and `gTTS` to generate the narration audio.

## Visual Design
· Theme: Deep space atmosphere with a dark blue background (#050914).
· Outer Wheel: A golden seven-spoke wheel rotating clockwise (4 degrees per second), with chronological anchors on the rim (3967 BCE, 2370 BCE, 607 BCE, 1914 CE).
· Inner Wheel: A blue twelve-spoke wheel rotating counter-clockwise (8 degrees per second).
· Center Core: A sanctuary motif labeled "1027 BCE" that pulses when the narration reaches that date.

## Narration Beats (45 Seconds)
· Beat 0 (0.0s - 7.5s): "Ezekiel saw wheels within wheels, turning beneath a vast expanse."
· Beat 1 (7.5s - 15.0s): "The Mastercore framework begins at thirty-nine sixty-seven B.C.E., creation's proposed dawn."
· Beat 2 (15.0s - 22.5s): "At twenty-three seventy B.C.E., it places the Flood, a watershed of judgment."
· Beat 3 (22.5s - 30.0s): "Ten twenty-seven B.C.E.: the Temple at the wheel's center, the axis of the pattern."
· Beat 4 (30.0s - 37.5s): "Six-oh-seven B.C.E.: a proposed prophetic anchor for Jerusalem's fall."
· Beat 5 (37.5s - 45.0s): "Nineteen fourteen C.E. completes the proposed pattern of appointed times."

## Audio Mix
· The narration is generated by Google Text-to-Speech.
· An ambient drone plays underneath (55 Hz sub, 110 Hz root, 165 Hz fifth, and a 660 Hz shimmer), fading in over 2.5 seconds and out over 3 seconds.
