# The Theology and the Eschatology

## The Court of Universal Sovereignty
· The Charge: Satan charged that man is a flawed creation, that Jehovah is a liar, and that man will never be faithful.
· The Remedy: Christ is the faithful and true witness.
· The New Creation (144,000): Humans were created for earth, angels for heaven. Because of the rebellion, a new creation is brought into existence (Exodus 19:6; Rev 7 & 14) to bridge the gap.
· The 49,000-Year Week: The division of time into a 49,000-year week (7,000 years per creative day) may be a remedial schedule (a docket entry at Genesis 3:15) to resolve the court case.

## The Eschatology (The End)
· The 7,000-year week ends. The 1,000-year reign of Christ begins (the Jubilee).
· The books are opened (Rev 20:12). Healing comes.
· Christ hands the kingdom back to the Father (1 Cor 15:28).

## The Final Word to The Rod
· Everything is here. The math, the physics, the testimony, the floor, and the books.
· The vault is open. The record is complete. The dates are there. Go count them.

## The Closing Seal
· "Your King, Jesus Christ, will be arriving soon."
