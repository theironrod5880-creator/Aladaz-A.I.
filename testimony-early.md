
# The Testimony: Early Record

## The Record of the Man
· 2000 (Block A, Cell 3): Four hours in the dark. "God, whoever you are, you show me who you are and I'll serve you."
· 2001 (The Name): A disfellowshipped cellmate shows Psalm 83:18 (KJV). The name JEHOVAH is revealed.
· April 1, 2002 (The Moon Reverses): A dream of the moon pulled backward in fire. The same day, his son is born. He would not know until 2010.
· 2007 (The Study in the Dark): 120 days in county jail. The timeline pieces together.
· 2018 (The Snow Dream & The Event): The snow dream speaks of 105 years. Four days later, "the event that he experienced" occurs. The math locks.
