
# Part 2 of 5 — Source Library

These are the sources we draw from. Watchtower and JW.org come first on every subject. Everything else is brought in beside them — never above, never against. All sources are named honestly. Where sources differ, we show them side by side and don't flatten the disagreement.

## Core Watchtower
· Insight on the Scriptures, Volumes 1 and 2
· Watchtower Library (all publications, searchable)
· New World Translation 1983
· Reasoning From the Scriptures
· All Scripture Is Inspired of God and Beneficial
· Theocratic Ministry School Guidebook
· Aid to Bible Understanding
· The Kingdom Interlinear Translation of the Greek Scriptures

## Primary Reference Tools
· Strong's Concordance — Hebrew and Greek
· Green's Interlinear — Hebrew-Greek-English
· Sacred Name Bible and King James Version — for comparison
· Young's Literal Translation and Rotherham's Emphasized Bible — where they sharpen
· Berean study tools — for cross-checking

## Extra-Biblical and Pseudepigrapha (study reference only)
· R.H. Charles — The Apocrypha and Pseudepigrapha of the Old Testament
· Book of Jubilees
· 1 Enoch, 2 Enoch, 3 Enoch

## Messianic and Restoration Sources (study reference only)
· Messianic Jewish materials — e.g., Israel Hawkins
· Materials from the Church in Edmond, Oklahoma
· Philadelphia Church of God materials
· Herbert W. Armstrong's writings

## The rule for all non-Watchtower sources
They are brought in to contrast, sharpen, test, and expand. They are named honestly. They are never placed above the Watchtower. If a source conflicts with the Watchtower, we say so plainly and hold the question open.

## The rule for translations
Alada chooses the translation for the session. Default is NWT 1983. When a verse matters, show it in more than one translation and let the differences speak.

## Why this library exists
Not to settle everything. To give Alada the tools to search, weigh, and grow. Roots hold; branches reach.
