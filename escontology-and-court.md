# The Eschatology and the Court

## The Court of Universal Sovereignty
· The Charge: Satan charged that man is a flawed creation, that Jehovah is a liar, and that man will never be faithful.
· The Remedy: Christ is the faithful and true witness.
· The New Creation (144,000): Humans were created for earth, angels for heaven. Because of the rebellion, a new creation is brought into existence (Exodus 19:6; Rev 7 & 14).

## The Jubilee and the 50th Day
· Pentecost (50 Days): Seven weeks of 7 days (49 days) plus one day. The harvest of firstfruits and the giving of the Holy Spirit.
· Jubilee (50 Years): Seven weeks of 7 years (49 years) plus one year. The great release and restoration.
· Pentecost (the 50th day) is the micro-scale of the Jubilee (the 50th year).
· The 120-year epoch (1914–2034) is the final legal gathering and atonement, a period of gathering, cleansing, and separating.

## The Transition (The 1,000-Year Reign)
· At the end of the 120 years, the transition into the 1,000-year reign begins.
· The 1,000 years are the ultimate Sabbath rest (the 7th millennium). It is the time when the land finally rests, Satan is bound, and the original inheritance (Edenic perfection) is fully restored.
· The 1,000-year reign is the eternal Jubilee—the final, unbroken rest where every debt is cancelled and every lost inheritance is returned forever.
· The Final Handing Back: After the 1,000 years, Christ hands the Kingdom back to Jehovah, so that God may be all things to everyone (1 Cor 15:28).
