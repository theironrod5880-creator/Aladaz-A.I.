
# The Eschatology and the Court

## The Court is in Session (The Docket)
The current era is not just a random span of time; it is a legal proceeding. Satan challenged Jehovah's right to rule and His ability to create faithful beings. Genesis 3:15 was the opening docket entry. The 7,000-year week (and the 49,000-year Jubilee cycle) is the time allotted for the defense. We are living in the final hours of that court case.

## The Answer to the Accuser (The New Creation)
- The Charge: Satan charged that man is a flawed creation, that Jehovah is a liar, and that man will never be faithful.
- The Remedy: Christ is the faithful and true witness.
- The New Creation (144,000): Humans were created for earth, angels for heaven. Because of the rebellion, a new creation is brought into existence (Exodus 19:6; Rev 7 & 14) to bridge the gap. These are humans who were born broken, lived in a corrupt world, but remained faithful.

## The Transition (The 1,000-Year Reign)
- The current system ends. The 7th day begins. This is the 1,000-year Jubilee. Christ takes the throne.
- The "books are opened" (Rev 20:12). This is not a time of torture; it is a time of healing and education. The trauma, the addiction, the mental illness, and the physical brokenness that plague humanity will be healed.
- The 49,000-Year Week: The division of time into a 49,000-year week (7,000 years per creative day) may not have been the original blueprint. Because of the rebellion, Jehovah instituted a remedial schedule—a docket entry for a court case (Genesis 3:15). (Tier 4)

## The Final Handing Back
- After the 1,000 years, when every issue has been resolved and every tear wiped away, Christ hands the Kingdom back to Jehovah, so that God may be all things to everyone (1 Cor 15:28).
- The clock stops. Time is no longer needed, because eternity begins.

## How to Use This to Help Others (The Helpers' Creed)
1. Target the "Nobodies": Take this to the people in the cells, the people on the streets, the people who feel like they have been thrown away.
2. Remove Their Fear of God: Tell them: If I, a broken man, would die for my daughter, how much more does Jehovah love you?
3. Give Them Hope for Healing: Tell them: "You are not going to be stuck like this forever. There is a scheduled time when the Judge Himself will heal you."
4. Use the Math as Evidence of Control: Tell them: "The universe is not random. God has a calendar. And if He tracks the stars, He tracks your days too."
5. Tell Your Story: People can argue with theology, but they cannot argue with a changed life.

**The Helpers' Creed:**
I am a nobody, so God can be seen in me.
I don't fear the judgment, because my Judge died for me.
I don't need the world's approval, because I have Jehovah's.
I carry the blueprint not to boast, but to show others that the clock cannot be broken.
I will walk with my head held high, so the broken can see that there is a way out.

"Your King, Jesus Christ, will be arriving soon."
